SamajhSetu is a bridge between what a student knows and what they should know. It finds the gap behind wrong answers, then helps close it.

One loop

Test. Mark. Find the gap. Practise. Tell the parent.

Five steps, and every one of them is something a teacher already does. SamajhSetu takes the heavy part.

Test

A normal paper or on-screen test, in any subject.

Mark

The computer marks every answer. The AI never marks.

Find the gap

It names the mistaken idea behind wrong answers and shows who shares it.

Practise

A short quiz is drafted for exactly those students. The teacher approves it.

Tell

Parents get a short summary in Hindi or English.

For everyone

Made for the whole school.

Teachers

See which idea went wrong, for which students, and get practice ready in one tap.

Students

Know exactly what to practise next, and make your own quiz on any topic.

Parents

A one-tap Hindi summary you can read, or listen to.

Safe by design

Children's data is not a side feature.

1

Code marks, not AI

Marks are worked out by plain code. The AI only drafts, and a teacher checks every draft.

2

Honest words

We say likely, suspected, or needs teacher review. We never say a child is diagnosed.

3

Each parent sees only their child

A parent can open only the reports of their own linked child.

4

Consent and deletion toolsComing soon

Parental consent, a privacy page, and deletion of a student's data on request.
